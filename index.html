  How auto-connect works

This app links to the shop automatically in the background, there is nothing to type or scan. If you and the shop are using the same device or browser (for example testing on one laptop), the link is instant. On separate phones over the open internet, this static, no-backend site can sync every post the moment your app opens and while it stays open, and true always-on live sync between two different phones needs a small relay service in a future version.
